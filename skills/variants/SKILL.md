# Variants

The operator asks for variants to compare different answers by eye. A variant that keeps the
previous variant's fields, type, spacing and structure and moves one thing gives the operator
nothing to pick between. Plan the differences on an axis grid before you draw.

## Axes

| Axis | Positions to choose from |
|---|---|
| Encoding | Plain text; an icon with its value; a chip or coloured dot; a bar, sparkline or other small chart |
| Content | Every field; only the fields the operator decides from; the rest behind a disclosure or the detail |
| Lead | The field a reader sees first: title, status, figure, date or owner |
| Structure | Table; list; cards; groups under headings; list beside a detail pane |
| Typeface | The theme's family; a second family for headings or figures; tabular or mono figures; a different weight and size scale |
| Density | Compact rows (32 to 36 px); regular (44 to 48 px); roomy (64 px and up), with matching gaps |
| Margins | Full width; a capped column; wide side margins; edge-to-edge rows without a card |

Keep the theme's colours. Typeface, scale, spacing and structure are open.

## Steps

1. List the screen's fields and the decision the operator makes from the screen.
2. Write the axis grid: one row per variant, one column per axis, one position in each cell.
3. Check the grid against these criteria, and change rows until it meets every one:
   - Each pair of variants differs on at least four axes.
   - At least one variant moves a text field to an icon, chip or chart.
   - At least one variant hides a field that the others show.
   - At least one variant changes the typeface or the type scale.
   - The variants use at least two densities and two margin positions.
4. Name each variant by its main idea in two to four words.
5. Draw each variant from its grid row, with the same invented data in every variant.
6. Present the grid above the variants. Under each variant, write one line on what it gives up.
   Recommend one.

The variants are done when the grid meets every criterion in step 3 and each drawn variant matches
its row.
