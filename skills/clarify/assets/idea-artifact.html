Why

Example: the weekly list takes ten minutes to read because each row repeats its group. Grouping the rows under one heading each cuts it to one screen.

Increments

  1. Example: group the rows

    The list shows one heading per group and its rows under it. Accepted when a group with three rows shows one heading and three rows.

    You open the weekly list on your phone and read it in one screen.

  2. Example: remember the open groups

    A group you close stays closed after a reload. Accepted when a closed group is still closed after a reload.
