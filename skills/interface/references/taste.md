# Taste

What the operator approved and rejected, and why. Add an entry for each reaction, with its date.
Move a reaction that has become a working rule into the reference for its area. Holds no reference
images, only what they show.

## Wanted

- **Soft, premium surfaces.** Pills and large corners of 24 to 32 px, white or near-white cards
  floating on a tinted or gradient page, wide and soft low-opacity shadows, few borders, generous
  spacing, a geometric sans font, and one dark or saturated primary action. Source: operator's five
  reference screens, 2026-11-02: an insurance app, a food app, an agents app, a tasks app and a
  finance dashboard.
- **Readable captions, unlike the references.** Their palest captions fail contrast, so keep the look
  and meet the text contrast rule. Source: design call, 2026-11-02.

## Rejected

- **Hard, loud looks.** Heavy black outlines, hard offset shadows, terminal and print pastiche, and
  bevels are "too aggressive", and the operator would never use them. Source: operator, 2026-11-02,
  on the Print, Terminal, Soft, Brutal and Bevel Button pilot.

## Waiting for a verdict

- Graphite, Ember and Aura as base themes. Source: `pepdevs/material` plan 0001.
