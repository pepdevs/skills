# Navigation and detail

## Structure

- **Overview first, then zoom and filter, then detail on demand.** Pressing an item opens its detail
  with the matching filter and tab already set. Source: `docs/research/layouts.md` (:143, :144,
  :212); `docs/research/figures.md` (:33).
- **Order destinations by how often they are used.** Source: `docs/research/layouts.md` (:43).
- **Carry entity, period and tab through navigation and the URL.** Back and reload return to the
  same view. Source: `docs/research/table.md` (:279); `docs/research/layouts.md` (:115).
- **Every view has a visible way out.** Source: impeccable: critique.md.
- **Leave out a Back link when breadcrumbs are present.** Source:
  `docs/research/navigation.md` (:80).
- **A collapsed sidebar is a labelled rail**, with a one-line label and count. Source:
  `docs/research/layouts.md` (:44, :69).

## Detail

- **On desktop, detail opens in a right panel beside the list.** The list keeps its place, scroll and
  filters. The panel closes by its cross, a press outside or Esc. Source: operator, decided
  2026-10-27 over impeccable: distill.md; `docs/research/layouts.md` (:197, :249).
- **On phone, detail takes the page, with Back.** See [phone](phone.md). Source: operator, decided
  2026-10-27; `docs/research/layouts.md` (:217, :249).
- **Use a modal last.** Prefer an inline edit or the detail panel. Source: impeccable: operate.md.
- **Show one overlay at a time.** Source: `docs/research/navigation.md` (:129 to :141).

## Controls

- **Use tabs for 2 to 5 distinct areas, and a segmented control to switch views of the same content.**
  Destination children handle routes. Source: `docs/research/navigation.md` (:13 to :19, :61,
  :67); `docs/research/collections.md` (:193).
- **Choose the control by option count**: a segmented control for 2 to 5 options with labels of 3 words or
  fewer, a checkbox list for 5 or fewer multiple choices, a select above that, with search above
  10. Never drop options to fit. Source: `docs/research/collections.md` (:137, :191, :312).
- **Put "All" first in a filter segmented control.** Source: `docs/research/collections.md` (:190).
- **One control per job.** The legend is the category filter: pressing a legend row isolates it.
  No separate category filter sits beside it. Source: `docs/research/charts.md` (:98);
  `docs/research/figures.md` (:163).
- **Offer ranges with presets.** A date can always be typed, and a range is confirmed with Apply.
  Source: `docs/research/inputs.md` (:255, :263, :298, :300).
- **Sections stay expanded.** Source: `docs/research/navigation.md` (:246).
